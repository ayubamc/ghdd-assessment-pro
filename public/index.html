Imtixaanku wuxuu ku bilaabmayaa sheeko/qoraal akhris oo ku qoran luqadda imtixaanka, ee ku dhisan manhajka. Su’aalahana waxay ku salaysan yihiin sheekadaas iyo objectives-ka manhajka.

🗓️ Lesson Plan + Lesson Note

Upload garee Scheme of Work-ka maaddada. AI-gu wuxuu ka diyaarinayaa qorshaha casharrada sannadka oo dhan iyo lesson note kooban oo la jaanqaadaya objectives-ka cashar kasta, isagoo raacaya qaabka sample-ka aad soo dirtay.

Muhiim: Scheme of Work ayaa ah isha ugu weyn. AI-gu waa inuu raacaa cutubyada, mawduucyada iyo kala-horraynta ku qoran. Natiijada dib u eeg ka hor inta aan la isticmaalin.

1. Scheme of Work — PDF toos ah (OCR looma baahna)

Halkan ka dooro PDF-ga Scheme of Work ee aad haysato. App-ku wuxuu akhrinayaa qoraalka ku jira PDF-ga si toos ah, OCR ma samaynayo, mana ku darayo liiska buugaagta OCR database-ka. PDF-gu waa inuu leeyahay qoraal la dooran karo; haddii uu yahay sawir la scan-gareeyey oo aan qoraal lahayn, app-ku wuu kuu sheegi doonaa.

Weli Scheme of Work PDF lama dooran.

2. Buugga maaddada — wuxuu hagayaa objectives, content iyo Lesson Note

Halkan ka dooro buugga maaddada ee hore OCR loogu sameeyey oo ku jira database-ka. PDF cusub halkan laguma upload-garaynayo. AI-gu wuxuu buuggaas ka soo qaadanayaa sharaxaadda, objectives-ka, tusaalooyinka, summary-ga iyo Lesson Note.

Marka hore soo qaado Scheme of Work iyo qoraalka buugga maaddada.

Waxa la soo saarayo

  • Scheme of Work: wuxuu go'aamiyaa kala-horraynta casharrada, topics-ka, term-ka iyo week-ga.
  • Buugga OCR: wuxuu bixiyaa objectives-ka casharka, sharaxaadda, tusaalooyinka, summary-ga iyo Lesson Note.
  • Lesson Plan: introduction, objectives, teaching methods, learning aids, evaluation iyo time management.
  • Jadwalka waqtiga casharka oo wadartiisu la mid tahay muddada aad gelisay.
